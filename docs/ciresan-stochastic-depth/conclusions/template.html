Can it classify—and can it lose layers?

Full-model accuracy and tolerance to deleting branches are separate outcomes. Switch checkpoints to see why the conclusion depends on the endpoint.

● Every branch kept◆ Two of four branches kept

Three-seed means and descriptive 95% t intervals (df=2). Two-branch accuracy averages all six masks; masks are not independent model replications. The branches use gain one in this audit. Keeping two branches retains 58.21% of nominal affine MACs averaged across all six masks; individual masks retain 33.14–83.28%. The stem and classifier still run, while crops add no affine MACs. Absolute-quality intervals are not a test of the paired deletion-damage contrast.
